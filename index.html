About Me

Physical & Analytical Chemistry • Photophysics & Nanomaterials • Ultrafast Spectroscopy • Optical System Development

I am a physical and analytical chemist with a multidisciplinary research background spanning materials chemistry, photochemistry, ultrafast spectroscopy, chemical imaging, environmental systems, and optical instrumentation.

My research is centered on understanding and applying light–matter interactions across diverse chemical systems. This includes the photochemical synthesis of functional nanomaterials, investigation of excited-state dynamics in lead-free semiconductor materials, studies of aerosol formation and transformation, and the development of advanced vibrational spectroscopy and microscopy platforms.

A major part of my work involves designing and integrating experimental systems, including O-PTIR and Raman microscopy, coherent Raman spectroscopy, ultrafast optical measurements, automated scanning, sensitive detection, and Python-based instrument control and data analysis.

I enjoy working at the interface of fundamental chemistry and practical problem-solving, with interests extending across academic research, university teaching, advanced materials characterization, analytical technology, and industrial R&D.

Research Areas

Research spanning functional materials, photochemistry, advanced spectroscopy, chemical imaging, and experimental system development.

Photochemical Nanomaterials

Light-driven synthesis and optical characterization of functional nanomaterials, with emphasis on controlling particle structure, morphology, and optical response through photochemical processes.

Ultrafast Spectroscopy & Photophysics

Investigation of excited-state dynamics, carrier relaxation, trapping, and recombination processes in functional materials using transient absorption and time-resolved optical spectroscopy.

O-PTIR & Raman Chemical Imaging

Development and application of complementary infrared and Raman microscopy for high-resolution chemical identification and imaging of polymers, microplastics, nanomaterials, and heterogeneous samples.

Coherent Raman Spectroscopy

Development of stimulated Raman scattering (SRS) and coherent anti-Stokes Raman scattering (CARS) systems using synchronized ultrafast laser sources for label-free vibrational spectroscopy and chemical analysis.

Environmental & Aerosol Photochemistry

Investigation of light-driven aerosol formation and particle growth under controlled atmospheric conditions, including transformation of industrially relevant gaseous pollutants into larger aerosol particles.

Optical System Development & Automation

Design and integration of laser-based optical systems, microscopy platforms, scanning methods, sensitive detection electronics, data acquisition, and Python-based instrument control and analysis.
