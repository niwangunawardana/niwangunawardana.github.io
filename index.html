About Me

Chemistry, spectroscopy and scientific instrumentation

I am a chemist specializing in physical and analytical chemistry, with research experience in laser-based spectroscopy, chemical imaging, photophysical characterization and advanced materials analysis.

My work combines optical instrumentation, spectroscopy, microscopy, scientific programming and materials characterization to investigate chemical systems ranging from polymers and microplastics to functional nanomaterials.

I am particularly interested in developing and applying advanced optical techniques that provide chemically specific information with high spatial and temporal resolution.

Research Areas

My research combines laser spectroscopy, microscopy, instrumentation and materials science.

O-PTIR Microscopy

Optical photothermal infrared microscopy combines infrared molecular absorption with visible-light detection to produce chemically specific images at spatial resolutions beyond conventional infrared microscopy.

SRS & CARS

Stimulated Raman scattering and coherent anti-Stokes Raman scattering use synchronized laser beams to probe molecular vibrations and generate chemically specific optical contrast without fluorescent labels.

Ultrafast Spectroscopy

Ultrafast spectroscopic methods allow the evolution of photoexcited materials to be followed over extremely short timescales, providing insight into excited-state dynamics, energy transfer and relaxation processes.

Raman Spectroscopy

Raman spectroscopy provides molecular fingerprints through vibrational scattering and enables chemical identification and structural characterization of a wide range of materials.

Nanomaterials & Photophysics

I investigate how material composition, structure and surface chemistry influence light absorption, emission, excited-state behavior and energy-loss pathways in functional materials.

Scientific Instrumentation

My work includes the development and integration of laser systems, microscopes, scanning platforms, lock-in detection, data acquisition systems and Python-based instrument control.

Featured Research Projects

Selected research projects and experimental developments

Multimodal O-PTIR & Raman Microscopy

Development of a microscopy platform combining infrared photothermal chemical imaging with Raman spectroscopy for complementary molecular characterization.

Nonlinear Raman Microscopy

Development and characterization of synchronized laser systems for stimulated Raman scattering and coherent anti-Stokes Raman scattering microscopy.

Photophysics of Nanomaterials

Spectroscopic investigation of light-emitting materials to understand excited-state dynamics, carrier trapping, recombination and energy-loss processes.
