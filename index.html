About Me

Chemistry, spectroscopy and scientific instrumentation

I am a chemist specializing in physical and analytical chemistry, with research experience in laser-based spectroscopy, chemical imaging, photophysical characterization and advanced materials analysis.


My work combines optical instrumentation, spectroscopy, microscopy, scientific programming and materials characterization to investigate chemical systems ranging from polymers and microplastics to nanomaterials.

Research Areas

My research combines laser spectroscopy, microscopy, instrumentation and materials science.

O-PTIR Microscopy

Chemical imaging using infrared absorption combined with visible-light photothermal detection, enabling spatial resolution beyond conventional infrared microscopy.

SRS & CARS

Nonlinear Raman techniques using synchronized lasers to probe molecular vibrations and generate chemically specific optical contrast without fluorescent labels.

Ultrafast Spectroscopy

Studying how photoexcited materials evolve over ultrafast timescales to understand excited-state dynamics, energy transfer and relaxation processes.

Raman Spectroscopy

Vibrational spectroscopy for chemical identification and molecular characterization of materials using their Raman scattering fingerprints.

Nanomaterials & Photophysics

Investigating how composition, structure and surface chemistry influence light emission, excited-state behavior and energy-loss pathways in functional materials.

Scientific Instrumentation

Development and integration of laser systems, microscopes, scanning platforms, lock-in detection and Python-based instrument control and data acquisition.

Featured Research Projects

Selected research projects and experimental developments

Multimodal O-PTIR & Raman Microscopy

Development of a microscopy platform combining infrared photothermal chemical imaging with Raman spectroscopy for complementary molecular characterization.

Nonlinear Raman Microscopy

Development and characterization of synchronized laser systems for stimulated Raman scattering and coherent anti-Stokes Raman spectroscopy.

Photophysics of Nanomaterials

Spectroscopic investigation of light-emitting materials to understand excited-state dynamics, trapping and recombination processes.
