System Development

I developed and integrated a home-built multimodal O-PTIR and Raman microscopy platform for spatially resolved chemical characterization.

My work included optical alignment, infrared excitation and visible-probe detection, lock-in based photothermal signal acquisition, microscope and motorized-stage integration, galvo-based scanning, Raman detection, and automated instrument control.

The system was designed to obtain complementary infrared absorption and Raman vibrational information from the same microscope platform, with particular emphasis on polymer and microplastic characterization.
